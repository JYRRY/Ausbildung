How it works

Three steps. Everything happens inside the Telegram bot.

1

Set your preferences

Pick the professions you want to apply for and the German states you can move to. Upload your CV. Link your own Gmail with an App Password.

2

The bot finds matches

JYRY AI watches public vacancies on the Bundesagentur für Arbeit board and matches them against your profile every day.

3

Applications go out

Each match is sent as a personalised email from your own Gmail account, paced through the day to look human. You can pause anytime.

Rate-limited by design. Each employer is contacted at most once per user. Your Gmail App Password is encrypted at rest. You stay in control — pause, edit, or cancel inside the bot at any time.
