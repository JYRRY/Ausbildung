How billing works

All paid subscriptions are processed by Paddle as the merchant of record. Paddle handles VAT/sales tax where applicable and issues your receipt. The charge will appear on your card statement as a Paddle transaction with reference to JYRY AI.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing cycle. You can upgrade to a higher tier at any time inside the bot — Paddle will charge the prorated difference and apply it immediately.

You can cancel at any time from inside the bot. Cancellation stops the next auto-renewal, but your plan stays active until the end of the current paid period — there is no cash refund for time already paid.
